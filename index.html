About & Profile

Indian Model, Actor & Fashion Entrepreneur

Imraan H. Mallick is an Indian model, actor, and fashion entrepreneur based in Kolkata. Born in Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir into a Bengali family, he studied at Delhi Public School, Srinagar. After completing his education, he moved to Kolkata to pursue modeling and acting.

He is known for leading roles in Bengali short films and is the founder of BSP Fashion (kids & menswear).

Languages: Hindi, Bengali, English, Kashmiri

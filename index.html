About & Model Profile

Indian Model, Actor & Fashion Entrepreneur based in Kolkata

Imran Hossain Mallick is an Indian model, actor, and fashion entrepreneur based in Kolkata. Born in Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir into a Bengali family, he studied at Delhi Public School, Srinagar. After completing his education, he moved to Kolkata where he started his professional journey in modeling and acting.

He is known for his leading roles in Bengali short films and his active contribution to the regional fashion and lifestyle industry. Imran is the founder of BSP Fashion, a kids and menswear clothing brand, and later expanded into BSP Tour and Travels focusing on Kashmir tourism.
